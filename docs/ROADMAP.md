What's coming to StoryLark.

## Near term

- Rate-limiting on sign-in and account creation for stronger account safety
- Preferences that follow your account: default playback mode, theme, and text size sync across your devices
- Sign in with Apple
- Selectable listen voices, with full narration across the whole library

## Later

- Richer library filters and search
- Reading stats: streaks, time read, finished shelves
- Refreshed story illustrations woven throughout the app
