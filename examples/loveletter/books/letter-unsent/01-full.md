label: The letter
---

*Found in the back of a desk drawer, addressed, stamped, never posted.*

I have started this four times and each time I have got as far as your name and
then gone and done something else with great urgency. The bathroom tap has been
fixed. The bathroom tap did not need fixing.

Here is what I wanted to say, and I am going to say it badly on purpose,
because if I write it well you will think I have practised, and I have, and I
would rather you didn't know.

You were wrong on Tuesday. Not about the thing we were arguing about — you were
right about that, obviously, you are always right about the small ones and it
is your least attractive quality. You were wrong when you said that I would be
fine. You said it kindly. You said it like a person handing back an umbrella.

---

I would like to be the kind of woman who is fine. I have watched them at close
range for years. They keep their letters short.

What I actually am is a woman who has been thinking, for six days, about the
moment on the platform when you turned round to check I was still there and I
was, and neither of us said anything about it, and then the train came.

So: I am not fine, and I am not asking you to do anything about it, and if you
are reading this then something has gone wrong with my resolve or right with my
courage and either way you should probably telephone.

Yours — and I have written and crossed out four different words here, and
*yours* is the one that survived —

A.

*End of letter.*
